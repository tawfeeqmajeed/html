About Us

Excellence in Technical Education

Empowering the next generation of engineers and technicians

Government Polytechnic College Anantnag is a premier technical education institution established in 1994 by the Government of Jammu & Kashmir. Located in the beautiful valley of Anantnag, our college has been at the forefront of technical education in the region.

We are committed to providing quality technical education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills, preparing our students for successful careers in engineering and technology. Our institution is approved by AICTE and affiliated with the Jammu & Kashmir Board of Technical Education.
